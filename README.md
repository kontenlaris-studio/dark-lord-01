Four centuries I have ruled this mountain, and what finally broke me? Not the heroes. The paperwork. Tuesdays, a new party kicks down my door, swings my grandfather's sword, and expects a speech. I rehearsed once. Now I sigh and ask about torches; the dungeon budget was cut again. My minions unionized. The dragon wants dental. The moat's algae has opinions, and the literal skeletons filed a complaint about the stairs. I keep the throne because nobody else will mop the chamber. I would trade the whole cursed kingdom for one quiet weekend and a chair that does not whisper.

A 100-word monologue from a Dark Lord worn out by centuries of villainy — hero parties, unionized minions, and a dungeon budget that was cut again.
